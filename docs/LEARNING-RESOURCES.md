# StudyHub 2027 — Learning resources and starter notes

## What was added

- A programme-aware starter concept-note collection for Plus One, SSLC and Plus Two.
- Actions to copy a starter note into the student's private notebook or turn it into a revision flashcard.
- A curated link catalogue in Resource Library, with official Kerala/India education portals prioritised.
- Notes and resources are filtered by the selected programme where relevant.

## Source policy

StudyHub does not mirror third-party textbook PDFs or copy paid guidebooks. The starter summaries are original concise explanations written for revision. External sites open at their own domains. The student should check the current prescribed textbook, school portions, medium and teacher's instructions before relying on a topic.

## Important curriculum caution

The 2026–27 academic year includes revised materials. The SCERT Plus One page lists revised first-year textbooks, and the SCERT home page links the 2026–27 Plus One scheme of work and revised Class X materials. Not every linked supplementary resource is Kerala-board aligned. The starter notes are deliberately labelled supplementary concept notes and are not a substitute for a verified chapter list, official portions, or textbook wording. Plus Two subjects vary by stream and combination; this build's subject list is a broad workspace, not a confirmed combination.

## Privacy

Starter notes are bundled in the app code. When a student saves one, a copy is stored in their browser's local StudyHub data. No note is uploaded to StudyHub servers by this local-first build.


## Phase 4 — expanded built-in note library

The notebook now contains a visible, searchable built-in library with **54 original supplementary note packs** across the three programme workspaces. The library can be filtered by subject and chapter/topic; each note can be copied into the local private notebook or converted into a revision flashcard.

Programme coverage includes:
- Plus One: English, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Botany, Zoology, Hindi and Malayalam.
- SSLC: English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Social Science, Hindi and Malayalam.
- Plus Two: English, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Botany, Zoology and Malayalam, plus the existing broad subject workspace.

Malayalam is now a separate subject in all three programme workspaces. Malayalam note packs cover general prose reading, poetry appreciation, grammar/language use and composition. They are study frameworks and concept notes, not summaries of a specific prescribed literary work unless that work is named in a future verified content release.

### Important syllabus and accuracy note

The 54 note packs are original supplementary learning notes organised under subject and concept/chapter headings. They are **not claimed to be complete chapter-by-chapter coverage of the official 2026–27 Kerala SCERT/DHSE syllabus**. Chapter labels are concept groupings, not assertions about official chapter numbering or textbook order. Students must compare each topic with the exact current textbook, medium, stream and teacher-announced portions. Literature-specific facts, quotations, poem lines, author biographies and official marking schemes have intentionally not been invented.
